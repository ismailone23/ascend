# Ascend privacy policy

_Last updated: 16 November 2026_

Ascend is a habit and goal tracker. Everything you put into it stays on your
phone.

## What Ascend stores

Your habits, goals, steps, progress logs, reminder times and settings. They are
saved in a database on your device and nowhere else.

## What Ascend collects

Nothing. Ascend has no account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and it
does not send your data to any server, including ours.

## Backups

When you export a backup, Ascend creates a file and hands it to the share
sheet. Where it goes from there (a file, a cloud drive, a message) is your
choice, and that service's own privacy policy applies to it. Importing a backup
reads only the file you pick.

## Permissions

- **Notifications**: to remind you of habits at the times you set.
- **Run at startup**: so reminders still arrive after your phone restarts.
- **Vibration**: for haptic feedback when you tap.

## Deleting your data

Delete habits and goals in the app, or uninstall Ascend to remove everything.

## Children

Ascend collects no data from anyone, including children.
