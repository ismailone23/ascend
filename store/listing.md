## App name (30)
Ascend: Habit & Goal Tracker

## Short description (80)
Build habits, keep streaks and break goals into steps. Private and offline.

## Full description (4000)
Ascend helps you show up every day, one small step at a time.

HABITS
• Pick the days each habit repeats and how many times a day it counts
• Swipe right to log, swipe left for details, hold to reorder
• Streaks follow your schedule, so a rest day never breaks one
• Set a streak goal and watch it fill up
• Reminders at the times you choose, with wording that changes

GOALS
• Break a big goal into a roadmap of small steps
• Tick steps off and see what comes next
• Give goals an icon and a target date

STATS
• This week at a glance, compared with last week
• Completion rate, perfect days and your best weekday
• A year-long heatmap of your progress

HOME-SCREEN WIDGETS
• Today's habits: tap one to log it
• Goals: tick off the current step straight from your home screen
• Today's progress and your streak
• Choose which habits or goals each widget shows

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no ads, no tracking
• Your data never leaves your phone
• Export a backup file whenever you like, and restore it on a new phone

Light and dark themes included.
