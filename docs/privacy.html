Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Ascend is a habit and goal tracker. Everything you put into it stays on your phone.

What Ascend stores

Your habits, goals, steps, progress logs, reminder times and settings. They are saved in a database on your device and nowhere else.

What Ascend collects

Nothing. Ascend has no account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and it does not send your data to any server, including ours. We do not sell or share any data, because we never have it.

Backups

When you export a backup, Ascend creates a file and hands it to the share sheet. Where it goes from there (a file, a cloud drive, a message) is your choice, and that service's own privacy policy applies to it. Importing a backup reads only the file you pick.

Permissions

  • Notifications: to remind you of habits at the times you set.
  • Run at startup: so reminders still arrive after your phone restarts.
  • Vibration: for haptic feedback when you tap.

Home-screen widgets

Widgets read the same on-device data to show your habits and goals. Nothing leaves the phone.

Deleting your data

See how to delete your data.

Children

Ascend collects no data from anyone, including children.
