Delete your data

Ascend has no accounts and no servers, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side: all your data lives only on your phone, and only you can remove it.

Delete some data

  • A habit: on the Home tab, swipe left on it to open its details, then tap Delete Habit. Its logs and reminders go with it.
  • A goal: on the Goals tab, open the goal and tap Delete goal. Its steps go with it.

Delete everything

  1. Open your phone's Settings → Apps → Ascend.
  2. Tap Storage → Clear storage to wipe all data and keep the app, or go back and tap Uninstall to remove the app and all its data.

Deletion is immediate and permanent. Nothing is kept anywhere else.

Backup files

Backup files you exported are yours and live wherever you saved them (your phone's files, a cloud drive, a chat). Delete them there.
